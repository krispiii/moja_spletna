About me

I’m a UX/UI designer. What catches me is the craft of it taking something rough and working on it, bit by bit, until it becomes a tool you can really use. I look for the spot where the user gets stuck, the pause, the many small questions, the not knowing where to go or what to click, the moment of doubt when they wonder if the product is really helping them. Then I find out what’s causing it and fix things until the user always knows what to do next.

Purposeful by Design

  • UX Research

    I use a structured process to understand users and their needs.

    Then I analyze their struggles to find pain points and ways to improve.

  • Design Process

    Design modular components that can be reused across the interface.

    Make and test mock-ups, then improve them based on feedback.

  • Post-Implementation Research

    Test designs to see what works and what doesn’t.

    Use numbers to measure results and understand how changes improve the product.
