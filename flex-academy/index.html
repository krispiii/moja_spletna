About the project

The project was a landing page for a coaching programme aimed at rental operators who want to grow their business. The main UX challenge was trust: the users are busy, sceptical of online courses, and quick to leave if something feels like a sales pitch. My task was to structure the page around their doubts, make the offer easy to understand, and design a simple booking flow for a free call, with a lighter option for people who weren’t ready to commit.

  • Industry

    Short-term rental coaching

    The product sits in the short-term rental industry, part of hospitality and property management, where operators rent or manage apartments and re-rent them to guests through platforms like Airbnb and Booking.com. More specifically, it’s a professional education and coaching product for that industry: rather than renting apartments or selling software, it helps rental operators learn to run and grow their business. It sits where hospitality, property management and business coaching meet.

  • The problem

    Earning trust, fast

    Rental operators who want to grow are sceptical of guru courses and too busy to read long sales pages, so the design had to build trust fast and make booking a free call feel easy and risk-free.

  • User groups

    Ambitious rental operators

    The page was designed for three types of rental operators. The side-hustler runs one to three apartments and wants to turn extra income into a real company. The stuck operator, the primary persona, runs several apartments but is held back by manual work, thin margins and no time to grow. The property professional, such as an agent, developer or landlord, wants to add a rental business without learning through trial and error. All three are ambitious, busy and sceptical of “guru” courses, and they compare every offer against free videos, cheap online courses and expensive masterminds.

Design process

The design process focused on making the page feel trustworthy, credible and professional from the first second to the last. I kept the page visual rather than text-heavy, so the story comes through without long reading. Most of the work was in the details: wording that speaks to sceptical operators, a layout that guides them through the page, and colours and type that feel warm but professional. I refined each part until the page worked as a whole.

How it is used

The website works as an information page that leads to one clear action: booking a free call. Visitors learn what the programme is, who it’s for and who teaches it, and get honest answers to their doubts along the way. At any point, they can book a free call with a founder to get advice and take the first step, through a short, clearly structured flow that removes hesitation and makes it easy to act right away.

Conclusion

The result is a landing page that explains a complex offer simply, builds trust without hype, and gives every visitor a clear way forward, whether that’s booking a free call or starting with the free guide.
