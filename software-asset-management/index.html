  • Industry

    Growing software stacks

    The product serves mid-size and large companies with growing software stacks, used mainly by IT admins, finance teams and department leads who manage access, licences, and costs. Each role needs a different depth of information, from a company-wide overview for decision-makers to precise, tool-level detail for admins handling day-to-day access.

  • The problem

    Scattered ownership and access

    Companies lose track of their software as it spreads across teams: ownership is unclear, access is scattered and no one has a full picture of who uses what or why. The challenge was to bring all of this into one structure that stays clear at every level, from the whole organisation down to a single tool, without overwhelming users with complexity.

  • User groups

    Admins, finance and team leads

    The main users are IT administrators, finance and procurement teams and department leads in mid-size and large companies. Each needs a different view: admins manage access and permissions tool by tool, finance tracks licences and spend and leads need a clear overview of what their teams use and who is responsible.

Design process

I designed the platform from scratch, starting with wireframes to map the hierarchy and then splitting the experience by role, so a user managing a single tool sees only what they need, while those overseeing more get the full depth. Every level stays connected: when one side makes a change, the others are updated, keeping information accurate and confirmed across the company-wide overview, departments and individual tools.

How it is used

The platform serves two types of users: admins who work in it daily and tool owners who only step in occasionally to add information such as users or pricing. When an owner makes an update, the admin is notified to review and confirm it, keeping both sides connected and the data accurate. Each sees a different depth and view, so owners get a quick, focused task, while admins keep the full overview.

Conclusion

This project brought order to a complex, scattered process, giving companies one clear place to manage their software, access and costs. By designing from scratch and shaping the experience around each role, the platform balances big-picture visibility with focused, tool-level detail. Connected workflows keep admins and tool owners aligned, so information stays accurate and everyone sees exactly what they need, nothing more, nothing less.
